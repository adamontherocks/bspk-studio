          Default going-train tooth counts are the 43 mm (19‴) example in Chapter VI: The Train: barrel 90 / center pinion 12, 80/10, 75/10, 75/8, escape wheel 16, giving 18,000 vph. The hour train (12/48 then 14/42), the minute drive (40/20 then 40/80 through a minute intermediate, 1:1), the layout and all sizes are designed here. Teeth are drawn involute; that text calls for epicyclic (cycloidal) teeth in watch trains.
